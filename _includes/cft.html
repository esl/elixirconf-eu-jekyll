CALL FOR TALKS


ElixirConf® EU 2027 in Lisbon is a two-track hybrid conference with 20- and 40-minute speaking slots including optional Q&A.

(We will also have a room for roundtable discussions but these are not included in the CFT process and will be run by the attendees themselves.)

Do you know Elixir or the BEAM and have something to share? Whether you are a core team member or a newcomer to the ecosystem, we'd love to hear from you!
Submit your talk proposal by 15 December.

Need talk ideas?

  • Phoenix and LiveView at scale: architecture, performance, testing and long-term maintenance.
  • Data pipelines and background processing with Broadway, Flow, Oban and Ecto.
  • Embedded and edge systems with Nerves and AtomVM, plus real-world case studies: scaling, ROI and migrating legacy systems.
