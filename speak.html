Speak at ElixirConf EU

Do you know Elixir or the BEAM and have something to share? Whether you are a core team member or a newcomer to the ecosystem, we would love to hear from you. The Call for Talks is open until 15 December.

Submit a talk

What speaking looks like

ElixirConf EU, Málaga 2026, photos by Piotr Kruszak

What makes a great talk

Do you know Elixir or the BEAM and have something to share? Talks that are technically deep, practical and focused on robust, maintainable, concurrent and distributed systems. Slots are 20 or 40 minutes, including optional Q&A.

How it works

1Submit your proposal on Sessionize by 15 December: title, abstract, level and a short bio.
2We review all proposals together and get back to you after 15 December.
3If you are selected, you get a free ticket and everything below. If not, we still send you something special afterwards.

What you get as a speaker

  • A free conference ticket.
  • A speakers' dinner the day before the conference, where you meet us (the organizers) and the other speakers.
  • A confirmation letter that you spoke at ElixirConf EU 2027, on request: just write to info@codesync.global.
  • A promo code to share with your network for conference tickets (Super Early Bird excluded), plus an invitation to all the accompanying events.

Why speak

Preparing a talk makes you understand your topic better. In two experiments, people who expected to teach a lesson remembered more of it afterwards than people who expected to be tested on it (Fiorella & Mayer, 2013, Contemporary Educational Psychology 38(4), 281-288, doi.org/10.1016/j.cedpsych.2013.06.001).

You also share your work with people who use Elixir and Erlang every day, meet the community and get feedback on your talk.
