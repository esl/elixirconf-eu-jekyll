Wendy is a freelance software developer passionate about functional programming, community speaker and host, and career-changer excited about helping others break into tech. She has co-hosted ElixirConf US and Clojure Conj, and spoken at Lambda World, Lambda Days, Clojure South, and Heart of Clojure. When she's not building with Elixir or speaking at conferences, she enjoys sewing, jazz dance, sports cars, and Disney.
