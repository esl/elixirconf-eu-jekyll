Overview

 

Discover even more reasons to love Elixir!

 

Welcome to ElixirConf EU, co-organised by ElixirConf and Code Sync. ElixirConf EU is the premiere Elixir conference in Europe, bringing the best speakers in the community together, for two days of first-rate Elixir talks and a day of tutorials.

  • Discover what’s new in Elixir
  • Learn new tools, techniques and best practices
  • Opportunities to network with Elixir professionals in a friendly environment
  • Stay current with Elixir developments in 2019 and beyond
  • Get first-class, industry recognised, Elixir training 
  • Find out how Elixir is being adopted by industry

 

Register now!

Early bird closes 19 Feb 2019!

Diversity Scheme

We are pleased to announce that ElixirConf EU 2019 is implementing a Diversity Scheme. The goal of the programme is to increase diversity of attendees and offer support to those in the tech community who would not otherwise be able to attend the conference. A committee will review applications and selection will be made on an individual basis.
